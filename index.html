// 01 — About Me

Engineer, builder,
problem-solver.

I'm Karabo Makgoba, a Mechatronics Engineer from the University of Cape Town. I live in the overlap between hardware and software — the place where a line of code makes a motor spin or a circuit come alive.

I schooled at the University of Cape Town, where I spent five years completing my Mechatronics degree. I got vacation work experience at Balancell, a company building intelligent energy-storage and battery-management systems, where I learned how these systems work and assisted in building test rigs for PCBs. I've also worked with UCT to create a digital twin for an electric vehicle's electric drive.

Other projects I've done include building a maze-solving micromouse and designing its power-management system. I've also designed the power architecture for an autonomous buoy meant to spend months in the ocean collecting data to help address the global-warming crisis.

My own projects include building web platforms that give high-schoolers access to over 1000 past papers and my own custom resources — because as much as I love engineering, I'm passionate about giving kids hope to do well in school.
